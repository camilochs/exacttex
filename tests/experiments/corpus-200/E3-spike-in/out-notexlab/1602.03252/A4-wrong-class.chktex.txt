Electrical resistance normalized to its room temperature value $R/R_{\rm{300K}}$ as a function of temperature $T$ data are shown in Fig.~\ref{fig:rhoHC}a for Zr$_2$Te$_2$P and Hf$_2$Te$_2$P, where the electrical current was applied in the basal $ab$-plane. While this is expected from the band structure calculations presented below, further angle resolved measurements will be useful to illustrate the degree of two dimensionality.
