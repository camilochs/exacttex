A bigger challenge is to accurately account for interactions between risk factors. Our model was developed by synthesizing risks for individual factors from large overviews of many studies, and large datasets which contain all the factors will be needed to fully explore interactions. These are not currently available but are likely to be so soon, as the risk factors used in our model are currently being collected in several large cohorts of patients.
